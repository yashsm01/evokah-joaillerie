The Beginning

Born in the Diamond
Capital of the World

Evokah Joaillerie was founded in 2015 in Surat, Gujarat — a city responsible for cutting over 90% of the world's diamonds. Growing up surrounded by the art of gem-craft, our founders saw a gap: the most technically flawless stones were being set in pieces that lacked soul.

So we started differently. Before sketching a single design, we sat down with couples, families, and individuals to hear their stories. The jewellery that emerged wasn't designed — it was revealed.

Today, that founding philosophy governs everything we do. Every stone we set carries meaning. Every metal we choose is traced to its ethical source. Every piece we deliver is accompanied by a story card — the story you told us.

The Craft

Where Science
Meets Artistry

Each Evokah piece passes through the hands of at least seven artisans before it reaches you. From the initial wax carving to the final hand-polish, every stage is performed by craftspeople who have dedicated their lives to the art of fine jewellery.

We combine traditional hand-finishing techniques that date back centuries with cutting-edge 3D design technology — because perfection demands both patience and precision.

  • ✦ Hand-engraving & milgrain work
  • ✦ Micro-pavé stone setting
  • ✦ Lost-wax casting & forge work
  • ✦ GIA-certified diamond grading
  • ✦ Hallmarked gold & platinum
